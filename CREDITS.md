## Not included, installed separately

These are dependencies, downloaded by the user from their authors:

- **SE98C** icon theme by nestoris (KDE Store 2330873, [github.com/nestoris/Win98SE](https://github.com/nestoris/Win98SE)). The installers copy a few of its icons into the login, lock and shut down screens on your machine.
- **ModernXP** cursor theme (KDE Store 2330874).
- **Reactionary ME** global theme by Die4Ever, for its panel layout idea and theme choices.
